What Does the AQI Mean?

Demystifying air quality categories established by National Air Quality Standards

0 - 50

Good

Minimal health impact. Air quality is considered satisfactory, and air pollution poses little or no risk.

51 - 100

Satisfactory

Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people. Moderate quality for the general public.

101 - 200

Moderate

May cause breathing discomfort to people with lung/heart disease, children, and older adults.

201 - 300

Poor

Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure. Limit outdoor exertion.

301 - 400

Very Poor

Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Significant risk for individuals with pre-existing conditions.

401 - 500+

Severe

Affects healthy people and seriously impacts those with existing diseases. Emergency health warnings.

Understanding Pollution Sources

Key drivers behind urban smog and seasonal pollution spikes in India

🚗

Vehicular Emissions

Old diesel engine exhausts, heavy traffic jams, and unserviced vehicles emit dense particulate matter, CO, and NOx.

🏗️

Construction & Dust

Uncovered demolition, building projects, and loose roadside dust contribute heavily to coarse PM10 levels.

🔥

Waste Burning

Open burning of municipal solid waste, plastic debris, and stubble releases toxic fumes and carcinogenic soot into urban air sheds.

🏭

Industrial Smoke

Power plants, brick kilns, and manufacturing units around city outskirts discharge sulfur dioxide and heavy particulate plumes.
